**New: live text in every overlay.** All eight built-in overlay styles now
show the words being transcribed while you speak, each drawn in its own look:
a TRANSCRIPT field on the Voice Card, a readout under the Waveform trace, a
log beside the Pulse dial, words in the Ocean Wave sky, a caption under Mono
Bars, a lyric line under Neon Spectrum, output lines in the Terminal and a
typed label under the Vinyl meter. When you stop talking, the finished
transcript replaces the live words before the overlay closes. Turn it on or
off in Settings → Visual & Feedback → **Show live transcript in overlay**
(on by default). With it off, every overlay looks exactly as before.

**New: corrections as you speak.** The newest words are drawn dimmer while
the speech engine may still correct them, and settle once two passes agree
on them.

**Fixed: live text froze after about ten seconds.** In custom overlays that
showed live text, the words stopped updating once you had been speaking for
about ten seconds and stayed frozen until the overlay closed. Live text now
keeps up however long you talk, and each update stays quick, because the
audio is finalized in pieces at the pauses between words instead of being
transcribed again from the start.

**For overlay authors.** Custom overlays can now show settled and
still-changing words separately (`data-voxctrl-live-committed` and
`data-voxctrl-live-tentative`), and can react to the setting and to the
final transcript through `--voxctrl-live-enabled` and `--voxctrl-live-final`.
The original `data-voxctrl-live-text` element keeps working. The `Custom/`
example shows how, and all six overlays in the
[overlays repository](https://github.com/JRufer/VoxCtrl-Scripts-and-Overlays)
have been updated. See [Overlays](https://github.com/JRufer/VoxCtrl/blob/master/docs/overlays.md#live-transcript-text).

### Notes

Live text runs extra transcription while you record. It is skipped
automatically with the remote speech engine and with medium or large Whisper
models running without a GPU, where it would fall behind your speech. The
words are on screen in screen shares, so switch the setting off if that
matters to you. An existing `Custom/` overlay folder is never changed; delete
it to get the new example.
