**New: cleanup styling per app.** S1-mini dictation cleanup can now use a
different style depending on where you're dictating: casual in Slack, formal
in your email client, and the usual style everywhere else. Add rules in
Settings → Post-Processing → **Styling per app**. **Detect app** gives you
three seconds to click into the app you want, then adds it for you. The
focused app is read from its window class on X11, Hyprland, Sway and KDE
(with `kdotool` installed), and from its program name on Windows. GNOME
can't report it, so the usual style always applies there.
