**Fixed: the Linux Vulkan + WebGPU AppImage in 0.8.0 would not start on
rolling-release distros such as Arch.**

It stopped at launch with `symbol lookup error: /usr/lib/libavif.so.16:
undefined symbol: SharpYuvConvertWithOptions`. The AppImage bundled its own
older copy of `libsharpyuv`, which took priority over the newer one on your
system, and your system's `libavif` needs the newer one. The bundled copy of
`libsharpyuv`, and of `libwebp` alongside it, is now only used when your
system has none, so yours is used whenever it exists.

If you downloaded `-linux-x86_64-vulkan-webgpu.AppImage` from 0.8.0 and it
would not start, download it again from this release. The plain
`-linux-x86_64-vulkan.AppImage` and the Windows installer are unaffected.
